About Volocal

Volocal is a fully local voice AI assistant for iOS. All processing -- speech-to-text, language model inference, and text-to-speech -- happens entirely on your device. No cloud services, no API keys, and no internet connection required. The language, speech recognition, and text-to-speech models are included in the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

What devices are supported?

Volocal requires iOS 17 or later running on a physical iPhone with a Neural Engine. It is not supported on the iOS Simulator or older devices that lack a Neural Engine.

How much storage space does Volocal need?

The installed app includes every model: about 2.13 GiB of model files (language model 1,327,696,992 bytes, speech recognition 224,238,533 bytes, text-to-speech 732,524,727 bytes) plus the app itself. There is no extra download on first launch. Plan for a bit over 2.2 GB of free space. The App Store uncompressed limit is 4 GB.

Does Volocal work offline?

Yes. The models are included in the app, so Volocal works offline from the first launch. No internet connection is needed to use any of its features.

How do I reset the models?

The models are part of the app. Delete and reinstall Volocal to restore them. There is no separate download. You can also go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Volocal and use "Offload App" or "Delete App".

The app is not responding or is very slow. What should I do?

On-device AI inference is resource-intensive. Try closing other apps to free up memory, then restart Volocal. If the problem persists, try restarting your iPhone.

Report a Bug

If you encounter a bug or unexpected behavior, please open an issue on GitHub: github.com/fikrikarim/volocal/issues. Include a description of the problem, your iPhone model, and your iOS version to help us investigate.
